03 · Proposed terms — pending approval

Cancellation & refunds

Request cancellation or raise a delivery issue by emailing aryipc@gmail.com, with your project reference. Please do not email card details.

  • Before work starts: the proposed policy is to refund prepaid service fees, less only third-party costs specifically approved in advance as non-recoverable.
  • After work starts: the proposed policy is to charge for documented work completed within the agreed scope and approved non-recoverable costs, and refund the unused balance. A written breakdown will be provided.
  • If I cannot deliver the agreed work: we would agree a remedy or refund for the undelivered portion. Delivery concerns are assessed against the agreed acceptance criteria.

No blanket “no refunds” rule is proposed. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Approved refunds would return via the original payment method where available; the processing estimate would be communicated when approved. These draft arrangements must be confirmed before this service page is launched or payment is accepted.
